# FamilyWealthOS
# Phase 7E – Product Hardening, Beta Readiness & User Experience Excellence

## Sprint Objective

Transition FamilyWealthOS from an engineering-complete platform into a polished, production-quality beta product.

This sprint is NOT about adding major new features.

It is about making every existing feature feel reliable, intuitive, delightful, and consistent.

------------------------------------------------------------

# PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

Every screen should answer three questions immediately:

1. What am I looking at?
2. Is everything healthy?
3. What should I do next?

Users should never feel lost.

The AI should proactively guide them.

------------------------------------------------------------

# SPRINT RULES

DO NOT redesign the architecture.

DO NOT rewrite calculation engines.

DO NOT change SQLite schema unless absolutely necessary.

DO NOT duplicate business logic.

Continue following:

AI explains.

Calculation Engines calculate.

Repositories retrieve data.

Application Services orchestrate.

Frontend renders.
