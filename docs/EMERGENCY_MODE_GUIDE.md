## 1. Emergency Console & Security Audit

The Emergency Mode Protocol provides immediate, secure access to critical succession information:
- **Professional Contacts**: CA, Lawyer, Family Doctor, Primary Executor.
- **Key Insurance Policies**: Term, Health, and Critical Illness policy numbers and sum assured.
- **Critical Documents**: Direct links to PAN, Policy Deeds, Property Documents.
- **Audit Protocol**: Every activation emits `EMERGENCY_MODE_ACCESS` event with correlation ID and timestamp in `estate_timeline`.
