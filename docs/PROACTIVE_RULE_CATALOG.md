## 2. Safety Gating: Completeness & Confidence

To prevent false-positive alarms or recommendations based on partial data, the observer enforces two mandatory safety gates before any trigger can be emitted:

1. **Domain Completeness Gate ($\ge 75\%$)**:
   - The relevant digital twin domain must possess a completeness score of at least 75%.
   - If completeness is $< 75\%$, trigger creation is suppressed and marked `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`.
2. **Deterministic Calculation Confidence Gate ($\ge 85\%$)**:
   - Calculation confidence must meet or exceed 85.0%.
   - Confidence is computed deterministically from source availability, point-in-time freshness, price recency, and calculation status.
   - **No AI / LLM Guesswork**: Confidence is never generated by an LLM prompt.

---

## 3. No-Fabrication Standard

Every recommendation and proposed action generated by the observer is strictly grounded in verifiable, authoritative evidence:
- **No Invented Products**: The observer never recommends specific third-party financial products.
- **No Phantom Policies/Nominees**: Actions only reference existing entities, assets, or documented legal gaps in the family's record.
- **Explicit Insufficient Data**: When required evidence is incomplete, the engine returns `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` rather than guessing.

## 4. Detailed Rule Specifications

### 1. `DRIFT_EQUITY_OVERWEIGHT`
- **Domain**: Portfolio Allocation
- **Formula**: $\text{Drift} = \left(\frac{\text{Equity Market Value}}{\text{Total Family Net Worth}} \times 100\right) - 60.0\%$
- **Evidence Payload**: `{ currentEquityPct, targetEquityPct: 60.0, driftPct, totalEquityValue }`
- **Suggested Action**: Review asset allocation and rebalance surplus gains into debt or liquid reserves.

### 2. `CONCENTRATION_SINGLE_STOCK`
- **Domain**: Portfolio Risk
- **Formula**: $\text{Concentration} = \left(\frac{\text{Max Single Holding Value}}{\text{Total Family Net Worth}} \times 100\right)$
- **Evidence Payload**: `{ maxHoldingName, maxHoldingVal, concentrationPct, thresholdPct: 20.0 }`
- **Suggested Action**: Diversify concentrated stock holding to reduce company-specific risk.

### 3. `INSURANCE_RENEWAL_DUE`
- **Domain**: Protection & Continuity
- **Formula**: $\text{Days Remaining} = \text{DateDiff}(\text{next\_premium\_due\_date}, \text{today})$
- **Evidence Payload**: `{ policyId, policyName, daysRemaining, dueDate }`
- **Suggested Action**: Review policy terms and execute premium payment before due date.

### 4. `PROTECTION_HLV_GAP`
- **Domain**: Protection Shield
- **Formula**: $\text{Gap} = \max(0, \text{Required HLV} - \text{Active Term Cover})$
- **Evidence Payload**: `{ currentCover, requiredCover, gapAmount }`
- **Suggested Action**: Explore pure term insurance coverage to bridge the family protection gap.

### 5. `EMERGENCY_FUND_DEFICIT`
- **Domain**: Liquidity & Reserves
- **Formula**: $\text{Runway Months} = \frac{\text{Liquid Reserves}}{\text{Monthly Non-Discretionary Burn}}$
- **Evidence Payload**: `{ liquidReserves, monthlyExpenses, monthsRunway }`
- **Suggested Action**: Allocate surplus monthly cashflow to high-yield liquid mutual funds or sweep deposits.

### 6. `EXCESS_IDLE_CASH`
- **Domain**: Liquidity & Cash Drag
- **Formula**: $\text{Runway Months} = \frac{\text{Liquid Savings Account Balances}}{\text{Monthly Household Expenses}}$
- **Evidence Payload**: `{ liquidReserves, monthlyExpenses, monthsRunway }`
- **Suggested Action**: Sweep idle cash into short-duration debt or systematic investment plans.

### 7. `GOAL_OFF_TRACK_DRIFT`
- **Domain**: Goals & Compounding
- **Formula**: $\text{Progress \%} = \left(\frac{\text{Current Allocated Amount}}{\text{Target Amount}} \times 100\right)$
- **Evidence Payload**: `{ goalId, goalName, targetAmount, currentAmount }`
- **Suggested Action**: Increase monthly SIP contribution or adjust goal timeline.

### 8. `TAX_80C_OPPORTUNITY`
- **Domain**: Tax Planning
- **Formula**: $\text{Headroom} = \max(0, ₹150,000 - \sum \text{80C Claimed Deductions})$
- **Evidence Payload**: `{ limit80C: 150000, current80C, headroom }`
- **Suggested Action**: Allocate towards tax-saving ELSS, PPF, or Term Life premiums before financial year end.

### 9. `ESTATE_NOMINEE_GAP`
- **Domain**: Estate & Succession
- **Formula**: $\text{Un-nominated Assets} = \text{Count}(\text{Assets without Active NOMINEE edge in KG})$
- **Evidence Payload**: `{ unNominatedCount, sampleAssetNames }`
- **Suggested Action**: Register legal nominees across demat, bank, and immovable properties to ensure smooth succession.
