# Family Command Center & Decision-Centric UX Architecture

## 1. Executive Concept: Decision-Centric vs. Card Clutter

The **Family Command Center** transforms the dashboard from a passive statistical dump of 30 widgets into an **actionable Family Office Operating Console**. 

It is designed around the 6 Fundamental Fiduciary Questions:
1. **What is happening?** $\to$ Net Worth, Asset Distribution, Cashflow Health.
2. **What changed?** $\to$ Market Movements, New Credits, Asset Drifts.
3. **What requires urgent attention?** $\to$ Uninsured Risks, Policy Renewals, Emergency Fund Deficits.
4. **What opportunities exist?** $\to$ 80C Tax Headroom, Tax-Loss Harvesting, Yield Optimization.
5. **What decisions are pending?** $\to$ Unassigned Nominees, Will Version Updates, Asset Rebalancing.
6. **What should I do next?** $\to$ Prioritized, 1-click executable action items.

## 2. Information Hierarchy & Cognitive Load Optimization

1. **The 3-Second Rule**: The user must immediately see their Net Worth, Health Index, and whether any urgent action is required within 3 seconds.
2. **Strict Maximum Action Constraint**: No more than 3 active high-priority recommendations are displayed simultaneously on the main screen to prevent choice paralysis.
3. **Progressive Disclosure**: Clicking any card or metric smoothly transitions into deep inspection drawers or dedicated workspace tabs (`/portfolio`, `/tax`, `/protection`, `/estate`, `/planning`).
4. **Theme Harmonization**: Fully adaptive light/dark mode with curated slate/indigo palettes and subtle status badging.
