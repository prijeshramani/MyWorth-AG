# Privacy & Data Governance Policy

## 1. Zero Cloud Tracking
- FamilyWealthOS operates 100% locally on your machine.
- No analytics trackers, telemetry beacons, or unauthorized usage monitors are included in the codebase.

## 2. Family Member Privacy Controls
- Data access is permission-aware based on family member assignments (`family_member_id`).
- Tax profiles, Form 16 documents, and e-filing JSON payloads are bound exclusively to the designated family member's PAN.
