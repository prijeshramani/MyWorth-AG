## 1. Retirement Corpus Requirements & Safe Withdrawal Rate (4% Rule)

1. **Future Expense Calculation**:
   $$\text{MonthlyExpense}_{\text{Retirement}} = (\text{CurrentExpenses} \times \text{PostRetirementRatio}) \times (1 + i)^{\text{YearsToRetirement}}$$
2. **Corpus Required (4% SWR)**:
   $$\text{Corpus}_{\text{Required}} = (\text{MonthlyExpense}_{\text{Retirement}} \times 12) \times 25$$
3. **Retirement Readiness Percentage**:
   $$\text{Readiness}_{\%} = \min\left(100\%, \frac{\text{Corpus}_{\text{Projected}}}{\text{Corpus}_{\text{Required}}} \times 100\right)$$
