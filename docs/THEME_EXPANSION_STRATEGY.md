## 1. Multi-Theme Strategy

Family Wealth OS supports 4 distinct visual theme presets:

1. **`Dark` (Default)**: Deep Navy Slate background (`#0b0f19`) with glassmorphic cards and crisp white text.
2. **`Light`**: Clean crisp white background (`#f8fafc`) with subtle slate borders and dark text.
3. **`System`**: Automatically inherits operating system appearance (`prefers-color-scheme`).
4. **`High Contrast`**: Pure black background (`#000000`) with high-contrast yellow/white borders for maximum accessibility.
