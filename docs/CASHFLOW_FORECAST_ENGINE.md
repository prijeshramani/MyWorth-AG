## 1. 10-Year & 30-Year Cashflow Forecasting

The `CashflowProjectionService` models:
- **Income Growth**: Monthly inflow indexed to salary growth rate (8% p.a.).
- **Expense Inflation**: Monthly outflow indexed to baseline inflation (6% p.a.).
- **Net Liquid Surplus**: Compounded annually in debt/liquid reserves.
