# User Personas & Fiduciary Archetypes

## 1. Persona Archetypes Matrix

| Persona Profile | Financial Complexity | Primary Problems | Core Goals | Typical Workflows | AI Advisory Requirements | Product Risk Factors |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Young Professional** *(Age 24-30, Single, Software/Finance)* | **Low – Moderate**: Salary, Demat stocks, Crypto/US Stocks, EPF, Credit cards. | Fragmented apps, lifestyle inflation, sub-optimal tax planning (New vs Old regime). | Early wealth creation, emergency fund setup, high CAGR capital growth. | Monthly statement imports, tracking portfolio net worth, tax-loss harvesting. | Proactive 80C alerts, SIP step-up suggestions, aggressive equity asset allocation. | Impatience with complex estate forms; needs ultra-fast, modern UX. |
| **2. Married Couple** *(Age 29-38, Dual Income No Kids / Early Family)* | **Moderate**: Dual salaries, joint home loan, multiple bank accounts, car EMI, Term cover. | Lack of consolidated joint net worth visibility, heavy EMI burden, coordination gaps. | Purchasing primary real estate, debt prepayment vs investing balance, joint emergency buffer. | Joint account syncing, joint tax optimization, EMI interest deduction tracking (Sec 24b). | Cashflow forecasting, joint HLV protection analysis, debt prepayment vs SIP simulations. | Confusion over asset ownership; requires clear member attribution. |
| **3. Family With Children** *(Age 35-48, Primary Earner + Spouse + 2 Kids)* | **High**: Equities, Mutual Funds, EPF/PPF/SSY, Term & Health Floater, School/College Goals. | Rising education inflation, underinsurance risk, lack of estate planning, nominee gaps. | Child higher education corpus (₹50L+), family medical shield, retirement visibility. | Sukanya Samriddhi tracking, annual insurance audits, goal rebalancing, Will drafting. | Automated HLV gap detection, education inflation stress testing, nominee reconciliation. | High anxiety around protection; needs crystal-clear, explainable advice. |
| **4. High Net Worth (HNI)** *(Age 42-60, Multi-Crore, HUF, Private Trust)* | **Very High**: Listed & unlisted equities, PMS/AIFs, Commercial properties, HUF accounts, Trusts. | Tax leakage (LTCG surcharges), complex estate transmission, regulatory compliance. | Wealth preservation, tax minimization, smooth multi-generational asset transmission. | Multi-entity reporting, Knowledge Graph relationship mapping, estate simulations, executive PDF exports. | Complex corporate action tracking, trust governance, capital gains tax harvesting strategies. | Demands 100% privacy (zero cloud data leaks) and absolute mathematical precision. |
| **5. Retired Household** *(Age 60+, Superannuation, Pension, Senior Citizen FDs)* | **Moderate – High**: Senior Citizen FDs, SCSS, Mutual Fund SWPs, Annuities, Health Insurance. | Outliving capital (longevity risk), healthcare inflation, complicated digital portals. | Predictable monthly cashflow (SWP), capital safety, seamless asset inheritance to heirs. | Tracking monthly pension/SWP inflows, fixed deposit maturity renewals, Will updates. | De-risking recommendations, conservative cashflow sustainability alerts, simple UI modes. | Vulnerable to technical jargon; requires high-contrast typography and clear text. |
