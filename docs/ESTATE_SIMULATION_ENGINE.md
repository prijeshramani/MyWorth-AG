## 1. Death Scenario Simulation Engine

The `EstateSimulationService` computes death scenario distribution models:
- **Distribution Trees**: Simulates percentage allocation across Spouse, Children, and Family Trusts.
- **Executor Action Plan**: Generates required legal steps (probate, municipal death certificate, nominee transmission).
- **Document Requirements**: Itemizes required legal deeds, ITR clearances, and identity proofs.
- **Tax Clearance**: Evaluates current Indian tax impact (₹0 estate duty under current IT Act).
