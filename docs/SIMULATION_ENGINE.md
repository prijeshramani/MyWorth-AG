# Pre-Packaged Simulation Templates

## Overview
FamilyWealthOS provides 6 pre-packaged simulation templates:

1. **Retirement Corpus Boost (`retirement_boost`)**: Simulates 10% annual SIP step-up to accelerate FIRE readiness.
2. **Tax Saving Re-investment (`tax_saving_optimization`)**: Re-invests Section 80C tax savings into ELSS funds.
3. **FIRE Early Retirement (`fire_early_retirement`)**: Stress-tests early retirement at age 50 under 8% inflation.
4. **Child Higher Education (`child_education_milestone`)**: Builds a ₹50 Lakh education corpus over 15 years.
5. **Home Purchase Down Payment (`home_purchase_downpayment`)**: Accumulates a ₹25 Lakh down payment over 5 years.
6. **Emergency Fund Shield (`emergency_fund_topup`)**: Parks 6 months of living expenses (₹3 Lakhs) in liquid debt.
