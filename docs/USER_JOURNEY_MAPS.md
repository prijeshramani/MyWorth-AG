# User Journey Maps & End-to-End Fiduciary Workflows

## 1. Journey 1: New Family Onboarding & Digital Office Setup

```
[ Step 1: Family Lineage ]
   User defines household members (Self, Spouse, Children, Parents), DOBs, and PAN identifiers.
         |
         v
[ Step 2: Bank & Broker Accounts ]
   Registers primary accounts (HDFC, SBI, Zerodha, Groww) and assigns primary account holders.
         |
         v
[ Step 3: 1-Click Statement Ingestion ]
   Uploads CAMS CAS PDF, Zerodha/AngelOne Excel, EPF Statement, and Bank Passbooks via Import Center.
         |
         v
[ Step 4: Asset Ledger & FD Valuation ]
   System normalizes holdings, maps ISINs, syncs Yahoo Finance prices, and calculates accrued FD interest.
         |
         v
[ Step 5: Insurance & Protection Shield ]
   Imports Life, Health, and Term policies; system automatically computes HLV target and flags gaps.
         |
         v
[ Step 6: Financial Goals & Milestones ]
   Defines Retirement age, Child Education horizon, and allocates specific asset pools to goals.
         |
         v
[ Step 7: Estate & Succession Blueprint ]
   Records Will status, verifies Nominee assignments across all assets, and nominates executors.
         |
         v
[ Step 8: Knowledge Graph Compilation & Command Center Activation ]
   System compiles complete Digital Twin, updates Graph Nodes/Edges, and generates AI Morning Briefing.
```

## 2. Journey 2: Comprehensive Retirement Planning & De-Risking

```
[ Step 1: Income & Expense Baseline ]
   Calculates net monthly household cashflow and trailing living expense baseline.
         |
         v
[ Step 2: Accumulation Portfolio Projection ]
   Simulates growth of EPF, PPF, NPS, Equity Mutual Funds, and Stocks up to target retirement age.
         |
         v
[ Step 3: Inflation & Longevity Stress Testing ]
   Applies 6.0% long-term inflation and runs Monte Carlo simulations for 30-year post-retirement horizon.
         |
         v
[ Step 4: What-If Simulation Sandbox ]
   User tests scenarios: *"What if I retire at 55 instead of 60?"* or *"What if equity return drops to 10%?"*.
         |
         v
[ Step 5: Fiduciary AI Action Plan ]
   Recommends exact monthly NPS/Mutual Fund SIP increase and automated de-risking glidepath.
```

## 3. Journey 3: Fiduciary Insurance & Protection Audit

```
[ Step 1: Consolidated Policy Inventory ]
   Aggregates all Term, Health Floater, LIC Endowment, and Critical Illness policies across members.
         |
         v
[ Step 2: Human Life Value (HLV) Mathematical Audit ]
   Calculates exact income replacement multiple + debt clearance load - liquid reserves.
         |
         v
[ Step 3: Health Insurance Adequacy Check ]
   Evaluates base sum insured vs rising healthcare inflation (e.g. minimum ₹25L family floater recommended).
         |
         v
[ Step 4: Nominee & Beneficiary Cross-Verification ]
   Verifies that every active policy has an explicit, surviving nominee matching the registered Will.
         |
         v
[ Step 5: Action & Renewal Execution ]
   Displays 30-day renewal alerts and 1-click term cover upgrade recommendations.
```

## 4. Journey 4: Emergency Survival & Estate Execution Mode

```
[ Step 1: Fiduciary Emergency Mode Activation ]
   Activated by authorized family member/executor in event of sudden demise or medical emergency.
         |
         v
[ Step 2: Instant Emergency Dossier Generation ]
   Produces consolidated PDF containing:
   - Primary bank accounts & emergency liquidity.
   - All active insurance policies with policy numbers, sum assured, and insurer claim toll-free numbers.
   - Registered Will copy, nominated executors, and asset-nominee distribution ledger.
         |
         v
[ Step 3: Step-by-Step Claim Settlement Checklist ]
   Provides interactive checklist for Death Certificate attestation, bank account claims, and insurance processing.
         |
         v
[ Step 4: Asset Succession & Transmission Ledger ]
   Tracks probate progress and legal asset transfer to beneficiaries in accordance with Indian succession laws.
```
