## 1. 8-Step Interactive Setup Workflow

1. **Step 1: Family Entity Setup**: Define family office name (e.g. `Sharma Family Office`).
2. **Step 2: Add Family Members**: Create members (Head, Spouse, Child, Parent) with PAN and Aadhaar linkage.
3. **Step 3: Bank Accounts**: Add primary savings bank accounts and Demat accounts.
4. **Step 4: Broker / Statement Import**: Import portfolio transactions via CAMS CAS, NSDL/CDSL, Excel, or Broker APIs.
5. **Step 5: Insurance Policies**: Input Term, Health, and Critical Illness policies with sum assured.
6. **Step 6: Configure Tax Profile**: Set financial year, residential status, and preferred tax regime (Old vs New).
7. **Step 7: Upload Documents**: Upload identity cards, insurance policies, and tax receipts into Document Vault.
8. **Step 8: Review Dashboard**: Complete onboarding and open the polished platform dashboard.
